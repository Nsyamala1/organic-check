What the digits mean

4011Four digits: grown conventionally.
94011Five digits starting with 9: organic.
84011Five digits starting with 8: genetically engineered (rarely used).

      What to look for on the pack

      Organic food in India is certified under one of two systems: NPOP, shown by the India Organic logo, or PGS-India, shown by the PGS-India Organic logo. Certified packs should also carry FSSAI's Jaivik Bharat logo.

      Search the company named after "Marketed by" or "Manufactured by". The registry matches company names only. It does not show whether a certificate is current, and a missing name does not mean a product is not organic.

      Source: Jaivik Bharat, FSSAI.

      Search Australia's organic directory

      Opens Australian Organic's directory of certified brands and businesses in a new tab.

      What to look for on the pack

      Australia has no mandatory standard for organic food sold locally, so the word "organic" on its own is not independently checked. Look for a certifier's logo and certificate number, such as Australian Certified Organic's "Bud".

      Certifiers approved by the Australian Government for organic exports include Australian Certified Organic (ACO), Bio-Dynamic Research Institute (BDRI), Organic Food Chain (OFC) and Southern Cross Certified (SXC).

      The directory lists businesses that chose to be included, so a missing name does not mean a product is not certified. Source: Department of Agriculture.
