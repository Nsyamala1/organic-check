Organic Check

Scan or type a barcode to see if a packaged food carries an organic label, which additives it has, and how processed it is. Use the produce tab for stickers and pesticide lists.

Try:

Data comes from Open Food Facts, which people edit, so a missing organic label can mean it was never entered. To confirm a brand is certified, search it in the USDA Organic Integrity Database.

Reading a produce sticker

4011Four digits: grown conventionally.
94011Five digits starting with 9: organic.
84011Five digits starting with 8: genetically engineered (rarely used).
